# Does RAG reduce hallucination / improve faithfulness? Empirical evidence, baselines, metrics

## In brief
- Search/retrieval augmentation on the same model measurably improves factuality: GPT-4 + FreshPrompt (retrieval from a search engine) gave +32.6% (Relaxed) and +49.0% (Strict, i.e. no-hallucination) accuracy over vanilla GPT-4 on FreshQA, human-evaluated [freshllms-2023].
- But RAG does not eliminate hallucination: on RAGTruth, all six RAG-tested LLMs (GPT-4/3.5, Llama-2 7B/13B/70B, Mistral-7B) still produced hallucinated spans (density 0.06–1.53 per 100 words; GPT-4 lowest, Mistral worst) [ragtruth-2024].
- RAGTruth's detector-guided selection reduced hallucination rate 52.4%→19.3% (Llama-2-7B/Mistral-7B) and 9.8%→4.8% (GPT-3.5/GPT-4) [ragtruth-2024].
- Vectara's HHEM-based leaderboard (updated 2026-10-22) scores RAG-style summarization faithfulness: hallucination rates range 1.8%–24.2% across ~90 models (GPT-4.1 5.6%, GPT-4o 9.6%, Llama-3.3-70B 4.1%, o3-pro 23.3%) [vectara-lb].
- Citation-based RAG is not guaranteed faithful: up to 57% of citations in RAG responses were post-rationalized, not genuine reliance [citfaith-ictir-2025].

## Findings
- **FreshLLMs (FreshQA), GPT-4/GPT-3.5/ChatGPT/PaLM/T5/FLAN, eval 2023-04-26.** Baseline: vanilla prompting, Self-Ask, Perplexity.AI; metric: two-mode human eval (Relaxed = answer correctness; Strict = no hallucination), 50K+ judgments. Quote: "our best GPT-4 + FreshPrompt variant yields an improvement of 32.6% and 49.0% accuracy over the vanilla GPT-4 on FreshQA under Relaxed and Strict, respectively" [freshllms-2023]. Also: without retrieval, "overall accuracy ranges from 0.8% to 32.0% under Strict" — the gap between modes measures hallucination. Why: direct controlled with/without-retrieval comparison on one model family.
- **RAGTruth (ACL 2024), RAG responses from GPT-4-0613, GPT-3.5-turbo-0613, Llama-2-7B/13B/70B-chat, Mistral-7B-Instruct.** ~18K manually annotated responses (QA, data-to-text, summarization); metric: hallucination density (spans/100 words). Quote: "OpenAI's two models demonstrated notably lower hallucination rates compared to others. Specifically, GPT-4-0613 exhibited the lowest hallucination frequency" and "the Mistral-7B-Instruct model generated the highest number of responses containing hallucinations" [ragtruth-2024]. Densities: GPT-4 QA 0.06, Llama-2-7B QA 0.59, Llama-2-13B data-to-text 1.53. Why: quantifies residual hallucination *under* RAG, per model.
- **RAGTruth mitigation experiment.** Baseline: random choice between two responses; metric: hallucination rate on 450-instance test set. Quote: "the first strategy reduced the hallucination rate by 21.6%, while the second strategy achieved a reduction of 63.2%" (52.4%→19.3%, Llama-2-7B-chat/Mistral-7B-Instruct); for GPT-3.5/GPT-4, "42.9% and 51.0%" (9.8%→4.8%) [ragtruth-2024]. Detection baselines: "GPT-4-turbo achieves only an average F1 score of 63.4%" vs fine-tuned Llama-2-13B "78.7% f1" [ragtruth-2024].
- **Self-RAG (ICLR 2024), Llama-2-based 7B/13B.** Baselines: ChatGPT, retrieval-augmented Llama2-chat; metric: factuality and citation accuracy on open-domain QA, reasoning, fact verification. Quote: "Self-Rag outperforms ChatGPT and retrieval-augmented Llama2-chat on Open-domain QA, reasoning and fact verification tasks, and it shows significant gains in improving factuality and citation accuracy for long-form generations relative to these models" [selfrag-2023]. Why: adaptive retrieval + self-critique beats fixed-context RAG on the same base model.
- **FActScore (EMNLP 2023), person biographies; models incl. InstructGPT, ChatGPT, retrieval-augmented PerplexityAI; GPT-4.** Metric: % of atomic facts supported by a knowledge source (human + automated via retrieval, <$2% error). Quote: "ChatGPT only achieves 58%" and "GPT-4 and ChatGPT are more factual than public models" [factscore-2023]. Why: the standard atomic-fact faithfulness metric; includes a retrieval-augmented system.
- **Vectara Hallucination Leaderboard (official benchmark, updated 2026-10-22).** Metric: HHEM-2.3 hallucination rate on 7,700+ document summaries (RAG-style). Quote: "LLMs are increasingly used in RAG... so this leaderboard is also a good indicator for the accuracy of the models when used in RAG or agentic systems" [vectara-lb]. Sample rates: antgroup/finix_s1_32b 1.8%, Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo 4.1%, Mistral-Large-2411 4.5%, GPT-4.1 5.6%, GPT-4o-2024-08-06 9.6%, Claude-Sonnet-4 10.3%, Grok-4-fast-reasoning 20.2%, o3-pro 23.3%, Phi-4-mini 23.5%. Why: time-stamped, model-specific RAG faithfulness numbers across Llama/Mistral/GPT/Claude.
- **Vectara FaithJudge paper (EMNLP Industry 2025).** Quote: "RAG aims to reduce hallucinations by grounding responses in external context, yet large language models (LLMs) still frequently introduce unsupported information or contradictions even when provided with relevant context" [vectara-paper-2025]. Why: A-source confirmation that RAG reduces but does not remove hallucination.
- **Citation faithfulness (ICTIR 2025, de Rijke et al.).** Metric: citation correctness vs. citation faithfulness (genuine reliance). Quote: "we reveal prevalent post-rationalization issues, finding that up to 57% of citations lack faithfulness" [citfaith-ictir-2025]. Why: attribution accuracy in RAG; correctness ≠ faithfulness.
- **FaithBench (NAACL 2025), 10 modern LLMs / 8 families, summarization.** Quote: "Our results show GPT-4o and GPT-3.5-Turbo produce the least hallucinations. However, most state-of-the-art hallucination detection models have near 50% accuracies on FaithBench" [faithbench-2025]. Why: recent, model-specific hallucination rates; detector limits.
- **RAG survey (2312.10997, ~6200 citations).** Quote: "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has emerged as a promising solution by incorporating knowledge from external databases. This enhances the accuracy and credibility of the generation" [rag-survey-2023]. Why: field-level framing (B).

## Conflicts
- Effect size vs. residual risk: FreshLLMs shows large relative gains (+49% Strict for GPT-4) [freshllms-2023], while RAGTruth and Vectara show substantial hallucination remains under RAG (0.06–1.53 spans/100 words; 1.8–24.2% rates) [ragtruth-2024][vectara-lb]. Not contradictory: gains are task/model-specific; RAG helps most on fast-changing knowledge, least where models ignore context.
- ICTIR finds up to 57% of RAG citations unfaithful (post-rationalized) [citfaith-ictir-2025], yet FaithBench finds GPT-4o/3.5 produce the least hallucinated summaries [faithbench-2025]: answer-level faithfulness is high, attribution-level is not.

## Gaps
- HaluEval (Li et al., EMNLP 2023) details not examined here; it evaluates LLM hallucination generally, not RAG-specifically.
- RAGTruth's Llama-2-70B fine-tuning-on-RAGTruth result is not captured in the quotes retained (only the 13B detector result).
- Vectara leaderboard has no paired with/without-retrieval baseline for the same model; FreshLLMs is the only controlled same-model comparison kept.
- FActScore's PerplexityAI (retrieval-augmented) numeric score was not extracted.
